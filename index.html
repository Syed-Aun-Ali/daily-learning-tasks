Our products & services

From single corrugated shippers to fully branded retail cartons, every job runs through the same quality-controlled process on our Sundar facility floor.

01

Corrugated Packaging

Shipping boxes, export crates and multi-ply corrugated cartons engineered for load-bearing strength and safe transit.

02

Custom Retail Boxes

Bespoke folding cartons and rigid boxes designed and die-cut to showcase your brand on the shelf.

03

Offset & Flexo Printing

High-resolution offset and flexographic print runs for cartons, labels and point-of-sale packaging.

04

Labels & Stickers

Product labels, security stickers and pack-level branding printed to match your artwork exactly.

05

Food & Beverage Packaging

Food-grade cartons and packaging built to hygiene and durability standards for FMCG and beverage brands.

06

Nationwide Delivery

Palletised, damage-checked dispatch from Lahore to clients across Pakistan on agreed production timelines.
