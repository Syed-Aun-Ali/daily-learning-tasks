# Fine Packages – Home Page (Practice Project)

A responsive front page built with **HTML** and **Bootstrap 5**, based on the website of **Fine Packages (Pvt) Ltd**, Pakistan's first ISO 9001 + 14001 certified printing and packaging company, based in Lahore.

> **Note:** This is a learning project. The company name, logo, text and images belong to Fine Packages (Pvt) Ltd. This copy is not the official website.

**Live demo:** https://syed-aun-ali.github.io/fine-packages-clone/

---

## About the company

Fine Packages designs and manufactures corrugated boxes, offset cartons, labels and custom retail packaging from its facility in Sundar Industrial Estate, Lahore. It has 26 years of production experience and over 200 active clients.

**Certifications shown on the page:** ISO 9001, ISO 45001, ISO 14001 and HACCP.

## Page sections

1. **Navbar** – logo, menu with an About dropdown, phone number and "Get a Quote" button
2. **Hero** – headline, intro text, call-to-action buttons, company stats and factory photo
3. **Info strip** – quick list of services
4. **Products & services** – six cards (corrugated packaging, custom retail boxes, offset & flexo printing, labels & stickers, food & beverage packaging, nationwide delivery)
5. **Mission & vision** – company commitment with two highlight boxes
6. **How we work** – five steps from consultation to support
7. **Certifications** – ISO and HACCP badges
8. **Testimonials** – three client reviews
9. **Trusted by** – customer logos
10. **Call to action** – "Ready to spec your next production run?"
11. **Footer** – company links, contact details and address

## Built with

- HTML5
- [Bootstrap 5.0.2](https://getbootstrap.com/) (loaded from a CDN)
- A small block of custom CSS (smooth scrolling and a hover lift effect on cards)

No build tools or installs are needed.

## How to run it

**Option 1 – open the file**
Double-click `index.html` and it opens in your browser.

**Option 2 – VS Code with Live Server**
1. Open the project folder in VS Code.
2. Install the **Live Server** extension.
3. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**. The page refreshes every time you save.
